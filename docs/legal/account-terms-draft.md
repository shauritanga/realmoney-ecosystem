# realMoney account terms — development draft

**Not approved for live customers. Use synthetic information during development.**

These draft terms describe account registration. They are not a loan agreement or
confirmation that a loan will be granted.

## Your account

You must meet realMoney's adult-customer policy, provide accurate identity and
address information, control the mobile number you register, and keep your
password and verification codes private. Do not register using another person's
identity or wallet. Contact the operator if information is incorrect or access is
compromised.

## Verification and applications

Identity and mobile-money ownership checks are required before applying. Providing
information does not guarantee approval. The operator must assess each application,
including the customer's stated income, expenses and existing repayment obligations.
A higher borrowing allowance is a maximum, not a promise to lend or a requirement
to borrow that amount.

## Separate loan terms

Before accepting any loan, review its principal, interest rate and period, fees,
total repayment, repayment dates, late-payment charges, and applicable cancellation
and complaints arrangements. Account registration is not acceptance of future loans.
Repayment must use the payment methods shown in the app. Never provide your
mobile-money PIN to realMoney staff or enter it into this registration form.

## Communications and records

Account and loan service communications are separate from optional marketing.
Keep your contact information current. The app records the terms version and the
time of acceptance. Read the privacy notice for information about data processing.

## Operator review required before publication

The operator must supply and approve its legal name, address, licence details,
support and complaints contacts, account closure arrangements, dispute process,
applicable jurisdiction, and any mandatory customer rights. These draft terms
must be reviewed against the actual business practices and applicable requirements.
